# Sample data

* `sample/sites.json` — six monitoring sites in Rudraprayag district at **approximate locality coordinates** (±1 km), demo thresholds, the rectangular pilot-area box, schematic river centrelines and reference places. No physical sensors exist at these points.
* `sample/incidents.json` — three **IMPORTED** summaries of publicly reported events (research publication and news reports, with source links, no casualty figures, not cross-checked against an official inventory) and six **SYNTHETIC** records titled `[SYNTHETIC]` that describe no real event.

Sensor telemetry is not stored here; it is generated by the simulator at seed time and labelled `SIMULATED`. All files are safe to redistribute. Do not add restricted datasets or personal data to this folder.
